- § Recall the notion of **non-repudiation** (as part of integrity) 

   - § Protection against an individual falsely denying having performed a particular action 

   - § Provides the capability to determine whether a given individual took a particular action such as creating information, sending a message, approving information, and receiving a message 

- § This is indeed a desirable property when signing a contract or e-commerce application 

- § In other situations, it may put privacy at risk as it may be the case of e-voting or offthe-record conversations 

- § The opposite of repudiation is **plausible deniability** aiming to ensure that an instance of communication between computer systems leaves behind no unequivocal evidence of its having taken place 

      - Notice that in this case, a security requirement is seen as a violation of a a privacy requirement and vice versa
